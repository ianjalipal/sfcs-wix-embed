Script to Screen Grant

Free entry. A 5–10 page original script. The school produces the winner with full kit, a student crew and post-production.

Opens 15 November 2026 and closes 20 December 2026.

The idea

A film school looks for new voices. Not only its students: anyone with a story. The school picks one script and turns it into a professionally produced short film, with its own equipment, its crews and its post-production.

Who can enter

Entry is free. No entry or handling fee, ever. Open to independent filmmakers, screenwriters and creative minds anywhere. You don't need to be an SFCS student. You must be 18 or older by the deadline. Under 18? Apply with written consent from a parent or guardian.

The script

5 to 10 pages, not counting the title page. A PDF in standard screenplay format: Courier 12pt, proper margins, sluglines and character names. Free tools like WriterDuet, StudioBinder or Celtx work well. In English, and completely your own work. Adaptations of novels, comics or existing characters are disqualified. Ideally, register your screenplay with the SWA.

Keep it shootable

The school pays for and shoots the winning film, so the script has to be possible to make. Scripts with these may be flagged or left out: Heavy CGI, green screen or complex VFX Period settings that need vintage cars, costumes or old architecture More than 3 locations. Keep it to 1–3 easy places: a flat, a shop, a park. Risky stunts or real weapons

What the winner gets

Your script goes into production, fully financed by the school. Cinema kit (camera, grip and lighting, field sound), a student crew in every key role, and full post: edit, DI colour and sound design. A “Written by” or “Story by” credit. If you live close enough and can show directing ability, you can pitch to co-direct with a student co-director. The film premieres at the annual SFCS Film Showcase.

The rights

You keep 100% ownership of your screenplay. The winner gives SFCS an exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to make the film and to screen, distribute and use it for education, promotion and festivals. The final decision rests with SFCS, which may change, pause or cancel the grant at any stage.

Questions, answered

Do I need to have made a film before?

No. You are welcome to apply even if you haven't made films. We also look for people with a background or portfolio in theatre, journalism, still photography, graphic design or any creative discipline.

What is the difference between Foundation and Intense?

Foundation is 3 practical months with a collaborative batch short film funded by the school. Intense is 3 months of deeper, professional training for only 5 students per batch, plus 3 months of placement or project work.

What does the Intense fee include?

Your graduation film: production, distribution, travel, shoot stay, food and all other film production expenses.

Is there a placement after the course?

Yes. After you complete an Intense course, we provide 3 months of internship or film project work.

What do I need to apply?

A 1–2 page personal statement, education certificates (10+2 or bachelor's) and a resume. Filmmaking applicants add a film critique and an original story idea; screenplay applicants send two writing pieces; cinematography applicants send a 1–5 minute showreel.

Where do classes happen?

On sets and locations. SFCS is a No Classroom film school, with campuses in Indore and Ahmedabad.
